## 2. The window's two false starts (kept as the record's own evidence)

1. **The 2304-row auto-fit drift.** The card's post-event idle floor *dropped* (more free VRAM): the chain's
   auto chunk fit read **2304 rows** (4.79 GiB free, half at most) — a shape outside every pre-registered
   arm's envelope (all baselines ran 2048) — and the relaunch's ttft-set arms hard-recovered there (the
   amdgpu boundary class, *intermittent*: `s3tip` and `c2` had run the same 2304-row shape clean minutes
   earlier; the prior lane's window-2 comment: "both hotfixed-tip ttft-set arms"; `summary.txt` kept one
   `PARSE-FAIL` line — the second arm was stopped before its parse). The pre-registered envelope was
   restored rather than chased: cap the fit, don't ride the drift.
2. **The wrong pin.** Take 2 set `COLI_VK_CHAIN_ROWS=2048` — the wrong seam: `v4c_rows` clamps it into the
   CPU's ≤128-row prefill class, a different regime entirely. Its one parsed leg: **800.714 s TTFT**
   (`summary.txt`, kept as the knob evidence). Stopped after it.
3. **Take 3 (the standing shape restored):** `COLI_VK_CHAIN_ROWS_MAX=2048` caps the auto fit at exactly the
   2048-row chunks every pre-registered baseline arm ran — the fit line reads "4.79 GiB free …
   COLI_VK_CHAIN_ROWS_MAX caps it at 2048" in all 13 legs. 12/12 arms + the DPROF rep clean.

## 5. D11 text-divergence counts (armed arms vs C0, the greedy channel)

C0's three reps are byte-identical (0/3 divergent). **9/9 armed reps diverge from the C0 text**, each arm
3/3 internally stable — the flips are deterministic per configuration, not noise:

- **G (3/3): the residency class.** Shares C0's first ~22 tokens, then flips: "…defined multiple times,
  *with each new definition overwriting the previous one*" (C0) → "*with the later definitions
  overwriting the earlier ones*" (G). The growth adds places without touching any resident's bytes — the
  flips come from experts that G holds on the device where C0 computes them on the CPU (different
  accumulation order, near-tie tokens) — exactly AH §4's count-band class.
- **F (3/3): the FA numerics class**, divergent from **token 1** ("Looking at this code…" vs "This code
  has…") — the tile's fp32/bf16 rounds shift the distribution everywhere.
- **GF (3/3): token 1** like F, and *not* F's text: the two share the opening sentence and item 1's start,
  then diverge again at item 1's tail (F: "…sum to a target using a hash map approach (Two Sum
  problem)." then a blank line; GF: "…sum to a target (Two Sum problem)" straight on to item 2, dropping
  the hash-map clause) — G's residency flips move F's own text: the levers are additive in time, not in
  tokens.

## 6. The institutional lesson — a model-scale smoke gate is owed

Every gate that blessed PW-D (unit bit-identity over all routes, packed_scales identity, tiny oracles
token-exact, the review's lossless verification) ran at geometries that cannot reach the model's resident
path — and the defect surfaced only when the manager's window spent a GPU-hour on the real model. The
lesson, priced: **any slice that changes a tier/chain *default* owes one model-scale smoke leg (~2 min,
a short generation on the real model) before merge** — it would have caught fmt 15 at
the PW-D merge instead of at the window. Recorded in CAMPAIGN; the gate itself is the manager's to stand
up.

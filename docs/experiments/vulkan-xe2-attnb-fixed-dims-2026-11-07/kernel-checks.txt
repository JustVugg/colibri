# Kernel-only checks of chain_attnb, stock (dev e24cd51a's shader) against this change, on the B60's second GPU (GPU 1 of the VM), Mesa ANV 26.0.8, 2026-11-06 19:07-20:00. attnbench is a replay harness written for this work (not part of the PR): it records the engine's own chain_attnb dispatches for Qwen3.6's attention (16 heads over 2 KV heads, head dim 256) and compares every output float. GPU 1 was shared with other jobs, so times are indicative; the subgroup-16 times are the best of 3 repetitions (timing.sh). Files, as written by the runs:

===== timing.sh
#!/bin/sh
# speed, new against stock (best of 3 after the bits run), Qwen3.6's attention
B=./attnbench
for kv in 1 0; do
  $B port.spv stock.spv S=255 last=16384 kv16=$kv budget=0 reps=3 stats=0 | grep -E "^(BITS|TIME)" | sed "s/^/S 255 last 16384 kv16 $kv one dispatch: /"
  for last in 16384 32768; do
    $B port.spv stock.spv S=4096 last=$last cap=32768 kv16=$kv budget=68719476736 reps=3 stats=0 | grep -E "^(CASE|BITS|TIME)" | sed "s/^/  /"
  done
done

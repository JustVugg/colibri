Quality: each text read with its true tokens, 1024 of them after a prompt of 512 tokens (prose, code, Chinese) or 64
(chat), as in the fast-experts record, whose per-token files the plain and fast-experts runs of prose, code and chat
here reproduce byte for byte (it has no Chinese text); one run per variant and text (with the same binary and settings
the numbers are the same in every run), in fast mode: plain decoding (QWEN36_MTP=0), the MTP head's drafts,
QWEN36_FAST_EXPERTS=5 without and with the drafts, all with the same binary (bin-pr11/ through launchers/<variant>/).
Each run was /usr/bin/time -l env -i PATH=/usr/bin:/bin HOME=$HOME SNAP=<model+head> TEACHER=1 BENCH_NLL=<file>
launchers/<variant>/qwen36-bench <text> <512|64> 1024, after the nominal thermal state and a 60 s rest, in the order
code, prose, chat, Chinese; the harness reads the first 20,480 bytes of prose and code, the first 6,144 of the chat
and all 15,073 of the Chinese text. BENCH_NLL writes one line per token: its index, its negative log-likelihood and 1
if the model's first choice was the text's token (quality/). The comparisons are paired, token by token: python3
compare_nll.py <a.nll> <b.nll> prints the mean change in percent of a's mean with its standard error, the top-1 change
in points with its standard error, and each quarter's change in percent of a's mean over the whole text. In each csv,
gen_first_ms is the first of the 1024 decode steps and gen_steady_tps the speed of the other 1023 (the README gives
all 1024: 1024 / (gen_first_ms / 1000 + 1023 / gen_steady_tps)), and expert_miss counts the experts read, the prompt's
included; the [qwen36 mtp] lines of the logs count the drafts and the steps without the head.

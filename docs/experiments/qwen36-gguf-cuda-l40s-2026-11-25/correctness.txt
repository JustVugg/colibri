Method

On each GPU, the benchmark saved the dev output and compared the bits of
all 248,320 FP32 logits after prefill and at each measured decode step.
All 17 workloads matched on all eight GPUs at the measured revisions.
The tables count the three retained trial runs. Each GPU has 6,360 matching
vectors (1,579,315,200 FP32 values). The timed dev and trial runs also have
identical logged logit and token fingerprints, NLL and top-1 for every row.
The fingerprints below come from the timing log. NLL and top-1 are the
benchmark's per-workload values at its logged precision.

Workload numbering: 1 cold 64, 2 sequential 512, 3 final 64, 4 cold 512,
5–9 long prompts 2,048 through 32,768, and 10–17 Chat 1 through Chat 8.

Measured revisions

L4, A100 and RTX PRO 6000 used the earlier GGUF implementation. RTX 3090,
RTX PRO 4000 and RTX 5090 were measured before the RTX 4090 cache change.
RTX 4090 and L40S include the change. See base.md for the timing protocol.

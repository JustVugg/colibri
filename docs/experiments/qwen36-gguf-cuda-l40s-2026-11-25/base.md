The L4, A100 and RTX PRO 6000 measurements used the earlier GGUF implementation. The RTX 3090, RTX PRO 4000 and RTX 5090 measurements preceded the RTX 4090 activation-cache adjustment. The RTX 4090 and L40S measurements include that adjustment. The physical RTX PRO 4000 was the standard edition. The SFF edition shares its profile.

## Timing method

- Each workload has three retained runs per engine. Warm-up runs are excluded. Throughput columns show the median of those runs. Change is the median of the three paired percentage changes. On L4, the two engines ran separate sequences on the same allocation and change is the percentage difference between the two medians.
- The benchmark preserves the 64 → 512 → 64 sequence and the eight-turn chat sequence. Cold 512 is independent. The long prompts run from 2,048 to 16,384 tokens, plus a 32,768-token prompt with 128 decode steps. Where 32,768 was collected separately, it is shown alongside the long prompts for comparison.
- Prefill throughput counts newly read tokens. Decode throughput excludes the separately timed first decode step. † marks timing variability or a persistent software thermal flag recorded by the benchmark. Flagged timings are included in the medians.

Quality: each text read with its true tokens, 1024 of them after a prompt of 512 tokens (prose, code, Chinese) or 64
(chat), one run per version and text (with the same binary and settings the numbers are the same in every run), with
the MTP head's drafts: QWEN36_FAST_EXPERTS=5 without and with QWEN36_FAST_GUESSED=4, the same binary (bin-pr13/
through launchers/pr13-fast5/ and launchers/pr13-guessed4/). Each run was /usr/bin/time -l env -i PATH=/usr/bin:/bin
HOME=$HOME SNAP=<model+head> TEACHER=1 BENCH_NLL=<file> launchers/<variant>/qwen36-bench <text> <512|64> 1024, after
the nominal thermal state and a 60 s rest, in the order code, prose, chat, Chinese, J = 5 first; the harness reads the
first 20,480 bytes of prose and code, the first 6,144 of the chat and all 15,073 of the Chinese text. BENCH_NLL writes
one line per token: its index, its negative log-likelihood and 1 if the model's first choice was the text's token
(quality/). The J = 5 files are byte for byte those of the speculative-decoding record's runs with the drafts and J =
5 (made with its code commit, c8fc51fc, below this one); quality/<text>-plain.nll are that record's runs of plain fast
decoding (every chosen expert, QWEN36_MTP=0), for the second comparison. The comparisons are paired, token by token:
python3 compare_nll.py <a.nll> <b.nll> prints the mean change in percent of a's mean with its standard error, the
top-1 change in points with its standard error, and each quarter's change in percent of a's mean over the whole text.
In each csv, gen_first_ms is the first of the 1024 decode steps and gen_steady_tps the speed of the other 1023 (the
README gives all 1024), and expert_miss counts the experts read, the prompt's included.

Exploratory runs behind the choice of six announced guesses, on 2026-10-06. They come from development builds of
the Metal graph (the work that became the pull requests above this one, not published) with two switches of their
own, on Qwen3.6-35B-A3B on the same M1, with the experts read from experts.qc. One run each, after the nominal
thermal state and a 60 s rest, so differences of a few percent are within their noise (five and six below are).
Every run's harness output and engine log are at the end of this file.

1. How often the guess of each rank was among the 8 experts the next layer chose (a build that prints a diagnostic
line when QWEN36_GUESS_DIAG=1; a 512-token prompt, then 128 greedy decode steps). "today's" is the guess this
branch makes (the next layer's router on this layer's residual), "from the input" another one that build tried; in
brackets, how many of the 8 chosen were among the guesses up to that rank, cumulative.

code (code.txt):
[qwen36 guess] 512 tokens: right by rank, today's/from the input (cumulative share of the 8 chosen): 1:97/99%(12/12) 2:94/97%(24/24) 3:91/95%(35/36) 4:86/90%(46/47) 5:78/83%(56/58) 6:67/72%(64/67) 7:56/60%(71/74) 8:41/47%(76/80) 9:32/32%(80/84) 10:22/22%(83/87) 11:18/17%(86/89) 12:14/12%(87/91)
prose (promessi_sposi.txt):
[qwen36 guess] 512 tokens: right by rank, today's/from the input (cumulative share of the 8 chosen): 1:98/99%(12/12) 2:97/98%(24/25) 3:95/97%(36/37) 4:92/94%(48/49) 5:86/90%(59/60) 6:77/81%(68/70) 7:65/69%(76/79) 8:49/52%(83/85) 9:32/32%(87/89) 10:22/21%(89/92) 11:16/15%(91/94) 12:12/10%(93/95)

2. Decode speed by how many guesses are announced (a build where QWEN36_AHEAD_N sets the count, beyond the top-k
too), code.txt, a 512-token prompt, 128 greedy decode steps, the engine's timers on (COLI_TIMERS=1). Decode speed is
steps 2 to 128 (the first step after the prompt is timed apart); guesses read and unused per generated token are
from the run's '[qwen36 mtp]' line ("unused": read for a layer that did not choose them). Run in the order 8, 12, 6,
10, 4, 0, 5, 3, 2.

development tree.

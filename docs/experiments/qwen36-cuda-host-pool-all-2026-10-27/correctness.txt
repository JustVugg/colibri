## 1. Where nothing moved, and the counter-proof (T4)
# The stack and the stack + this change agree at every length: before a chunk of 128 rows or more #2416's
#   follow completes the pool on the stack too. The stack + this change pools every expert at the start and
#   still holds them at the exit: the follow released none. Without the follow's check (the counter-proof)
#   the follow releases the residents at the first such chunk, and the exit's count is lower.

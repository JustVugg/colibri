INTERPRETATION / DECISION
High acceptance and fewer backbone forwards did not translate to request
throughput: both paired reads show MTP 12–16% slower. This agrees with the
historical pre-C18 result (plain 14.43–14.59 vs MTP 12.99–13.59 tok/s);
the C24 fused verify path has not flipped the sign. Keep `Q36_MTP` off.
Do not expand to IQ3/Q4_K_M or optimize the head absent a new mechanism
that materially reduces verify/draft cost. The single-prompt result is
directional, but the paired loss is larger than observed within-run
variation and consistent with prior evidence.

C25 DeltaNet prefill profiling verdict — IQ2_XXS
(2026-11-18; cold cache-off request, OMP=6)

Question
--------
How much of a representative cold prefill is DeltaNet, and which broad
DeltaNet stages warrant further study before any kernel change?

Workload and outcome
--------------------
- One fixed ledger prompt; 10,924 prompt tokens, 1 generated token.
- Cold prefill: 344.2 s (0 reused prompt tokens); TTFT 344.3 s.
- Component timers: DeltaNet 102.3 s (~29.7%), attention 54.4 s, MoE
  184.8 s, shared 12.9 s, router 7.8 s. These diagnostics overlap and
  are not additive.
- Request-scoped DeltaNet stage timers:
  input projections 56.0 s; convolution 10.7 s; recurrent state update
  plus norm 16.0 s; output projection 19.6 s.
- The input projection bucket includes the large qkv/z projections and
  the smaller alpha/beta projections. It is therefore a direction for
  further study, not a subprojection-level attribution.
- A 30-second macOS `sample` was captured during the request. Its samples
  were predominantly MoE; `gq_row_all` and `matmul_q_idot` were hot in the
  process but cannot be attributed to DeltaNet from this sample.

Verdict
-------
DeltaNet is material to cold-prefill time, and input projections are its
largest measured stage. No production optimization or kernel A/B was run:
the evidence does not isolate an individual input projection or establish
a correctness-preserving optimization opportunity / expected gain. The
profiling-only timers are gated by COLI_TIMERS and inactive by default, so
retain them for a future subprojection study. If pursuing that study, first
split qkv, z, alpha, and beta timings, then require bit-exact validation and
a representative A/B before adopting any kernel.
